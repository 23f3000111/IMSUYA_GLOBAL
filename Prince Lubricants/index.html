About Prince Lubricants

Since 1998, PRINCELUBE (ASIA) PTE LTD has been the exclusive licensee of the 'Prince Lubricants' brand in over 40 countries. For over two decades, Prince Lubricants has excelled in meeting the diverse lubricant needs of modern life — engineering and manufacturing high-performance lubricants and functional oils for the automotive, marine, and industrial sectors across three continents.

Proprietary Innovation

P-9 Ester Technology

🔬

Formidable Molecular Bonding

High-polarity P-9 molecules form an unbreakable bond with polar structural components — magnetized powerfully and robustly on all metal surfaces.

🌡️

Thermal Stress Resistance

Repulse thermal related stresses without a break. Maintains viscosity "stay-in-grade" at extremely high operating temperatures under continuous load.

♻️

Highest Antioxidant Protection

Dense oil film prevents rust damage. Natural ester antioxidant properties allow minimal oil degradation and extended engine maintenance life.
