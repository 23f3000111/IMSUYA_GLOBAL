01 · FAQ

Frequently asked.

Questions clients ask most before they pick up the phone.
01What is the duration of the listing agreement?+
A listing agreement in Malaysia is typically valid for three to six months, depending on the property and the agreed terms. Shah will discuss the most appropriate duration based on your property type, current market conditions, and your timeline goals.
02How do you figure out how much my house is worth?+
Shah performs a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) — reviewing recent transactions of similar properties in the same area, considering size, condition, tenure, facing, and proximity to amenities. The goal is a fair price that attracts serious buyers without leaving money on the table.
03How do you intend to market my house?+
Marketing includes professional photography, listings on major portals (PropertyGuru, iProperty, Mudah), social media posts across Instagram and Facebook, WhatsApp broadcast to a qualified buyer database, and direct outreach to known active buyers. Every listing stays current — no stale photos, no outdated prices.
04What are the taxes I need to pay before buying a property?+
When purchasing property in Malaysia, buyers pay Stamp Duty on the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) and loan documents. First-time buyers may qualify for exemptions on properties up to RM 500,000. Real Property Gains Tax (RPGT) applies to sellers, not buyers. Shah will walk you through the exact figures based on your specific purchase.
05What should I do to get my house ready for showings?+
Key steps include decluttering, deep cleaning, minor touch-ups (paint, fixtures), ensuring good lighting and ventilation, and removing overly personal items. Shah will advise on property-specific improvements that offer the best return — and what not to spend on unnecessarily.
06What should be the language of the registration document?+
In Malaysia, official property documents — including the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) and title deeds — are issued in Bahasa Malaysia, which is legally binding. English translations are sometimes provided alongside. Shah handles negotiations and explanations in both Bahasa and English.
07Will you have open houses?+
Open houses are arranged based on the property type and the seller's preference. For residential properties, a scheduled open day can attract multiple qualified buyers in a single session. For commercial listings, Shah typically arranges individual appointments. Both formats are discussed and agreed with the owner upfront.
08What is property registration?+
Property registration is the formal process of transferring ownership from seller to buyer through the Land Registry (Pejabat Tanah). After the SPA is signed and the full purchase price is settled, the lawyer files a Memorandum of Transfer (MOT) stamped with Stamp Duty, officially recording the new owner. The process typically takes two to four months after SPA signing.
