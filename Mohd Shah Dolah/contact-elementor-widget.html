01 · FAQ

Frequently asked.

Questions clients ask most before they pick up the phone.
01What is the duration of the listing agreement?+
A listing agreement in Malaysia is typically valid for three to six months, depending on the property and agreed terms. Shah will discuss the most appropriate duration based on your property type, current market conditions, and your timeline.
02How do you figure out how much my house is worth?+
Shah performs a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) — reviewing recent transactions of similar properties in the same area, considering size, condition, tenure, facing, and proximity to amenities. The goal is a fair price that attracts serious buyers.
03How do you intend to market my house?+
Marketing includes professional photography, listings on major portals (PropertyGuru, iProperty, Mudah), social media posts, WhatsApp broadcast to a qualified buyer database, and direct outreach to known active buyers. Every listing stays current.
04What are the taxes I need to pay before buying a property?+
Buyers pay Stamp Duty on the SPA and loan documents. First-time buyers may qualify for exemptions on properties up to RM 500,000. RPGT applies to sellers, not buyers. Shah will walk you through the exact figures.
05What should I do to get my house ready for showings?+
Key steps: declutter, deep clean, minor touch-ups (paint, fixtures), ensure good lighting. Shah will advise on property-specific improvements that offer the best return — and what not to spend on.
06What should be the language of the registration document?+
Official property documents in Malaysia — including the SPA and title deeds — are issued in Bahasa Malaysia, which is legally binding. Shah handles negotiations and explanations in both Bahasa and English.
07Will you have open houses?+
Open houses are arranged based on property type and seller preference. For residential properties, a scheduled open day attracts multiple qualified buyers. For commercial listings, individual appointments are typically used.
08What is property registration?+
Property registration is the formal transfer of ownership through the Land Registry (Pejabat Tanah). After SPA signing and full payment, a lawyer files a Memorandum of Transfer (MOT) stamped with Stamp Duty. Process takes 2–4 months.
